A Home Away From Home

A warm, secure and welcoming hostel designed for students coming from different places to learn, grow and live together in harmony.

About ARUVI Hostel

ARUVI Hostel was established in the year 2018 with the vision of providing comfortable, safe and affordable accommodation for students. Located in the heart of Kakkanad, Kochi, our hostel offers a peaceful environment that supports both academic focus and social interaction.

The hostel consists of a total of 75 well-maintained rooms. Each room is designed to accommodate three inmates, with the provision to add one guest if required. Hence, the maximum holding capacity of each room is four members.

Facilities & Services

To maintain a clean, healthy and comfortable living environment, the hostel employs dedicated cleaning staff for daily maintenance of rooms and common areas. Residents are provided with purified drinking water through water filters.

Recreational facilities include sports kits, a badminton court, television in the common room, and a spacious mess hall serving hygienic food. These facilities encourage relaxation, fitness and social interaction.

Hostel Rules

To ensure a safe and disciplined atmosphere, residents are required to strictly follow hostel rules. Maintaining cleanliness, respecting fellow inmates, adhering to entry timings and protecting hostel property are mandatory. Any violation of rules may result in disciplinary action.
