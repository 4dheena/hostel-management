Rules And Regulations

Guidelines meant for a happy and safe stay

All residents of Aruvi Girls Hostel are expected to follow the rules given below to ensure a safe, respectful, and disciplined living environment.

1. General Conduct

  • Residents must maintain discipline, decency, and mutual respect at all times.
  • Any form of misbehavior, harassment, or misconduct will invite strict action.
  • Hostel property must be handled with care. Damage will be recovered from the resident.

2. Accommodation

  • Hostel accommodation is provided only to registered residents.
  • Room allotment is at the discretion of hostel management.
  • Unauthorized room shifting or allowing outsiders to stay is strictly prohibited.

3. Entry & Exit (No Curfew Policy)

  • Aruvi Girls Hostel follows a No Curfew Policy.
  • Residents must record their entry and exit details every time.
  • Overnight stay outside the hostel must be informed in advance.
  • Misuse of the no-curfew facility may lead to individual restrictions.

4. Visitors

  • Visitors are allowed only in designated common areas.
  • Visitors are not permitted inside hostel rooms.
  • Residents are responsible for the behavior of their visitors.
  • Overnight stay of visitors is strictly prohibited.

5. Safety & Security

  • Residents must cooperate with hostel staff and security personnel.
  • Activities that compromise safety will be treated as serious violations.
  • The hostel is not responsible for incidents occurring outside the premises.

6. Cleanliness & Maintenance

  • Rooms and common areas must be kept clean and hygienic.
  • Waste must be disposed of only in designated bins.
  • Periodic inspections may be conducted by the management.

7. Prohibited Items & Activities

  • Alcohol, drugs, tobacco products, smoking, and vaping are strictly prohibited.
  • Gambling, illegal activities, and loud disturbances are not permitted.
  • Unauthorized electrical appliances are not allowed.

8. Discipline

  • Violation of rules may result in warning, fine, or disciplinary action.
  • Serious or repeated violations may lead to suspension or expulsion.
  • The decision of the hostel management shall be final.

By staying in the hostel, residents agree to abide by all the above rules.
